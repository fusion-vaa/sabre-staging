Table B - Single Engine Taxi and Warmup/Cooldown Times​

info

In real world operations, many of our fleets have a warmup time that differs between the first flight of the day, or an engine that has been shut down for a long amount of time, and flights where the engine has been run more recently and is thus not fully cold.

For the A320 family, for example, the real world guidance is for a 5 minute warmup period after a shutdown period of greater than 4 hours (2 hours on the A21N) or a 2 minute warmup period if less than that.

Because there is not a way to simulate this differentiation within a flight simulator, and as we assume that most simmers fly one flight per session and that most simmers start the flight from a "cold and dark" panel state, we have built our scorers as if every flight is the first flight of the day.

info

Some aircraft are able to do a single/selected engine taxi on any engine configuration, while others are only permitted to taxi in one configuration. vAMSYS does not support scoring single/selected engine taxi on multiple engine configurations, so we have built our scorers on the preferred single/selected engine taxi configuration and have asked vAMSYS to provide functionality to enable multiple engine configurations. If and when that is added, we will revisit our scorers and documentation to adjust.
